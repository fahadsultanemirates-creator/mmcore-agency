Referral Points Policy

Exactly how M&MCore Points are earned, what they are worth, how to spend them, and what will void them.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

What Points are

M&MCore Points are a loyalty credit. 1 Point = $1 of credit toward any M&MCore service.

  • Points are not money, not a security, and not an investment.
  • Points are never paid out as cash and cannot be withdrawn, sold, or transferred to another account.
  • Points have no value outside M&MCore and cannot be redeemed against a third party.
  • Points do not expire while your account is open and in good standing.
This is a rewards programme, not an earnings opportunity. Nothing here is a promise of income, and we make no representation that referring anyone will earn you a specific amount.

How Points are earned

Points are credited when a referred client's project is paid for in full and completed — never for signing up, never for clicking a link, and never for a referral who has not ordered anything.

L1

Someone you referred directly

You earn 20% of their project value in Points.

L2

Someone they referred

You earn 10% of that project's value in Points.

L3

Someone that person referred

You earn 5% of that project's value in Points.

  • "Project value" means the agreed price of the project, before any Points were applied to it and before any discount that was itself funded by Points.
  • Crediting applies to a referred client's first 3 completed projects only. Their fourth and later projects generate no Points up the chain.
  • A referred client also receives 10% of their own first 3 projects back in Points.
  • Points are credited once a project is fully paid and completed — not at the 30% upfront stage.

How Points are spent

  • Tick "Apply my M&MCore Points" when submitting a request, and your Points are deducted from that order's price automatically.
  • Points can cover up to 100% of a service price. If your balance is smaller than the price, the remainder is payable normally.
  • Points apply after any Business Pool or package discount.
  • Points spent are deducted immediately and recorded in your account history. If an order is cancelled before work starts, the Points are returned.

The rules we enforce

  • One account per person or business. Creating extra accounts to refer yourself is self-referral and voids the Points earned.
  • No referring yourself, your own other accounts, or anyone you control.
  • No paid ads on our brand name, no spam, and no misrepresenting what M&MCore does in order to get a signup.
  • If an order is refunded or charged back, any Points it generated are reversed — across all three levels.
  • We may withhold or reverse Points obtained through fraud, abuse, or a breach of these rules, and may close accounts that do it repeatedly.

Closing your account

Unspent Points are forfeited when an account is closed, by you or by us. They are not refundable and not convertible to cash at any point.

Changes to this programme

We may change the referral percentages, the 3-project limit, or these rules at any time. Changes apply going forward only — Points already credited to your balance stay credited. If we ever discontinue the programme entirely, we will give at least 30 days' notice and you will be able to spend your existing balance during that window.

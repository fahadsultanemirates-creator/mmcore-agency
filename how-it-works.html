The dashboard, explained

How M&MCore actually works

One dashboard, seven tabs — nothing to guess about what happens after you sign up.

01

My Projects — where you land first

This is the first thing you see after logging in. Every project — active, waiting on your review, or already delivered — shows up here with a clear status, so you're never messaging us just to ask where things stand.

  • Status at a glance: In Progress, Awaiting your review, Revision requested, or Delivered
  • Open any project for the full detail — files, notes, and the conversation behind it
  • Built so a support ticket is rarely needed — the answer is usually already on the page
02

New Request — where a project actually starts

Describe what you need in your own words — no forms, no jargon. An agent asks a couple of follow-up questions to pin down the scope, then shows you a clear written summary and price to confirm before anything is charged.

  • One standard tier per service — the price shown when you pick a task is the price, no upsell ladder. Any discount you qualify for is already in that number.
  • Nothing starts until you approve the scope in writing — that protects you from surprises and us from misunderstandings
  • Once confirmed and paid, your project is named automatically and shows up under My Projects
  • A real person reviews every confirmed request personally — while our automated project manager is still being refined, nothing runs unsupervised
03

Starter Package & Marketing — the two bundled routes

Two tabs sit beside New Request for the orders that don't need scoping, because their contents are already fixed.

  • Starter Package — the $69 bundle for a business starting from nothing. Once its payment clears, every additional service you order is 50% off, permanently.
  • Marketing — the monthly AI marketing packages, billed for each month in advance rather than on the 30/70 split. Cancel any time before the next month starts.
  • Individual marketing services can also be picked à la carte from New Request, same as everything else
04

Billing — everything in one place

Pay directly from the dashboard — no separate invoicing portal to chase. One-off project work follows the same split every time: 30% upfront to start, 70% once the finished work is delivered and approved. Monthly services and marketing packages are billed in full for each month, in advance — there's no completion to hold a balance against.

We take payment in USDT on BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain). Place an order and the exact amount, the address and a QR code appear immediately; send it, message us the transaction hash, and a person confirms it — usually within a few hours.

The Billing tab keeps the full history: every payment, its stage, its status, and any Points you applied.

05

Referrals — your chain, your Points, your history

Your referral link, everyone who joined through it at all three levels, how much you've earned, and a full ledger of every Point credited or spent. Points come straight off your next order — tick one box when you submit a request. See how the programme works →

06

Mint — the assistant that helps you scope it

Not sure what you actually need, or what it should cost? Mint knows the full price list and how we work, remembers your previous conversations, and will help you shape a request before you submit it. When something needs real judgment, it puts a person in front of you rather than guessing.

07

Business Pool — for clients who keep coming back

Cross $5,000 in lifetime spend and you're automatically moved to a separate, elevated experience — faster delivery, a dedicated human manager, and 20% off every service, applied to your prices the moment you qualify. Read the full breakdown →

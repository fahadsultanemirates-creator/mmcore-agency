The Referral Program

Share your link. When the people you refer — and the people they refer — get work done, you earn M&MCore Points.

What are M&MCore Points?

1 Point equals $1 of credit toward any M&MCore service. Points aren't cash and can't be cashed out — they're a discount on future work, usable by you or on someone else's order. They never expire.

This is a rewards program, not an investment vehicle. Points are only earned once real, paid work is completed — never just for signing up or referring someone who hasn't ordered anything yet.

Three levels, decreasing rewards

L1

Someone you referred directly

You earn 20% of their task value in Points, on their first 3 completed tasks. They get 10% off those same tasks.

L2

Someone they referred

You earn 10% of that task's value in Points, on their first 3 completed tasks.

L3

Someone that person referred

You earn 5% of that task's value in Points, on their first 3 completed tasks.

A worked example

Scenario

You refer Sara. Sara refers Ali. Ali refers Nadia.

Nadia orders a $200 website and it gets completed.

What happens

Ali (referred Nadia directly, Level 1 from Nadia's side) earns $40 in Points (20% of $200).

Sara (Level 2 from Nadia's side) earns $20 in Points (10% of $200).

You (Level 3 from Nadia's side) earn $10 in Points (5% of $200).

This repeats for Nadia's first 3 completed tasks — after that, her further tasks stop generating Points up the chain.

Finding your link

Every account gets a unique referral link, right there in your dashboard. A dedicated Referrals view shows your direct and indirect referrals at every level, plus your current Points balance.

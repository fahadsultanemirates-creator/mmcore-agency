The Referral Program

Share your link. When the people you refer — and the people they refer — get work done, you earn M&MCore Points.

What are M&MCore Points?

1 Point equals $1 of credit toward any M&MCore service. Tick "Apply my M&MCore Points" when you submit a request and your balance comes straight off the price — Points can cover up to 100% of an order. They aren't cash, can't be cashed out, and never expire while your account is open.

This is a rewards program, not an investment vehicle. Points are only earned once real, paid work is completed — never just for signing up or referring someone who hasn't ordered anything yet.

Three levels, decreasing rewards

L1

Someone you referred directly

You earn 20% of their project value in Points, on their first 3 completed projects. They also earn 10% of those same projects back in Points.

L2

Someone they referred

You earn 10% of that project's value in Points, on their first 3 completed projects.

L3

Someone that person referred

You earn 5% of that project's value in Points, on their first 3 completed projects.

A worked example

Scenario

You refer Sara. Sara refers Ali. Ali refers Nadia.

Nadia orders a $200 website and it gets completed.

What happens

Ali (referred Nadia directly, Level 1 from Nadia's side) earns $40 in Points (20% of $200).

Sara (Level 2 from Nadia's side) earns $20 in Points (10% of $200).

You (Level 3 from Nadia's side) earn $10 in Points (5% of $200).

Nadia herself earns $20 in Points (10% of her own project).

This repeats for Nadia's first 3 completed projects — after that, her further projects stop generating Points up the chain. Points are credited once a project is fully paid and completed, not at the 30% upfront stage.

Finding your link

Every account gets a unique referral link, right there in your dashboard. The Referrals tab shows everyone in your chain at all three levels, how many Points you have earned, your current balance, and a full history of every Point credited or spent.

The complete rules — what voids a referral, what happens on a refund, and what happens if you close your account — are in the Referral Points Policy.

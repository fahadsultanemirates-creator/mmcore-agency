How we handle your data

Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, who touches it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Who we are

M&MCore Agency ("M&MCore", "we", "us") provides AI-run business services — websites, design, marketing, bookkeeping, reports, and custom AI agents. M&MCore operates under the AgenticCore umbrella. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use this website and dashboard, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

For any privacy question, or to exercise any of the rights below, contact us on Telegram at @mmcore_support.

What we collect

  • Account data — your name, email address, and a hashed password, created when you sign up. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Referral data — the referral code you signed up with (if any), your own referral code, and which accounts you referred.
  • Request and project data — the service you selected, what you wrote describing the job, any files you attached, the agreed price, and the status of the work.
  • Billing data — amounts, payment stage (upfront or final), payment status, and any Points you applied. We never see or store your card details or your wallet's private keys. Crypto payments are settled on-chain; we record only the amount and a transaction reference you give us.
  • Conversation data — if you chat with our assistant on this site, in the dashboard, or on Telegram, we store the transcript so the assistant remembers your context and so a human can pick the conversation up.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs kept by our hosting and database providers (IP address, browser type, timestamps), used for security and debugging.
We do not run advertising trackers, third-party analytics pixels, or cross-site profiling on this site. The only cookie-equivalent we set is the login session your browser needs to keep you signed in.

Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and to let you log in
  • To scope, price, deliver, and support the work you ordered
  • To calculate Business Pool eligibility and referral Points
  • To take and reconcile payment
  • To answer your questions and hand you to a human when needed
  • To meet our legal, accounting, and anti-fraud obligations

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Who processes it for us

We use a small set of service providers, each handling only what they need to:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage for your account, requests, and attachments
  • Netlify — website hosting and delivery
  • xAI — the model behind our AI assistant; your chat messages are sent to it to generate a reply
  • PayRam — payment processing, where a hosted payment link is used
  • Telegram — where you choose to contact us or your manager there

These providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on the provider's own standard contractual safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Account, request, project, and billing records: for as long as your account is open, and afterwards for as long as we are required to keep financial records (typically up to 7 years)
  • Uploaded attachments: for the life of the project, then until you ask us to delete them
  • Chat transcripts: up to 24 months
  • Server logs: typically 30–90 days, per our providers' retention settings

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate (you can edit your own name from your account at any time)
  • Delete your account and associated data, subject to records we must legally retain
  • Stop processing your data for a particular purpose, or object to it

Message @mmcore_support and we will respond within 30 days. Deleting your account permanently forfeits any unspent M&MCore Points — see the Referral Points Policy.

Security

Your data sits behind row-level database security, so one account can never read another's requests, projects, billing, or files. Uploaded attachments live in a private bucket reachable only by you and our staff. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and never visible to us.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as quickly as we reasonably can.

Children

This service is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects you meaningfully, tell you in the dashboard or by email.

This policy describes our actual practices in plain language. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a lawyer reviewing your obligations in the jurisdictions you operate in.

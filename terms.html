Terms of Service

Scope, payment, delivery, revisions, refunds, ownership, and liability — the rules every M&MCore order runs on.

Last updated: 15 September 2026

These terms govern every order placed with M&MCore. The payment and delivery rules you were asked to read before placing a request are section 3 and section 4 below.
01

Agreement

By creating an account or placing an order with M&MCore Agency ("M&MCore", "we"), you ("the client", "you") agree to these terms. If you are ordering on behalf of a company, you confirm you are authorised to bind it.

M&MCore operates under the AgenticCore umbrella and shares its delivery infrastructure. These terms are between you and M&MCore.

02

Scope of work

  • Every order is defined by the service, task type, and written description captured when you submit a request, plus anything additionally confirmed in writing before work starts.
  • Prices are set by our published price list. The price shown to you at submission is the price for that scope.
  • Anything not in that written scope is a new order at its own price. We will tell you before doing it — we do not add charges silently.
  • A real person reviews every confirmed request before any agent begins work.
We build and deliver using AI agents, with human oversight. You are buying a finished deliverable, not agent time or a quantity of credits.
03

Payment

  • Every service and package follows the same split: 30% upfront to begin work, and the remaining 70% on completion. There is no separate pricing model for packages.
  • Work begins once the 30% is received. It does not begin before.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Crypto payments are settled at the amount quoted in USD at the time of payment; any network or exchange fee is yours.
  • Monthly and retainer services are billed for each month in advance and continue until you cancel. Cancel any time before the next month begins and you will not be billed again.
  • Any M&MCore Points you apply are deducted from the order total before the 30/70 split is calculated. See the Referral Points Policy.
  • If the final 70% is not paid within 30 days of work being presented for review, we may suspend delivery and archive the project. Nothing is handed over until the balance is settled.
04

Delivery & turnaround

  • Simple services are typically delivered within 24–48 hours.
  • Heavier builds — full websites, multi-agent frameworks — can take several weeks. Indicative ranges are on the services page.
  • Some services are ongoing and monthly rather than a single delivery.
  • Turnaround estimates are estimates, not guarantees. They assume you respond to questions and supply the material we need. Delays caused by waiting on you extend the timeline by at least the same amount.
05

Review, revisions & handover

  • Before final payment, finished work is shown to you for review only. That is not handover.
  • Every order includes 2 free revision rounds. A round means one consolidated set of change requests, within the original scope.
  • Changes that alter the agreed scope are not revisions — they are a new order, quoted before we do anything.
  • Revisions beyond the two included rounds are billed separately, at the price of the relevant catalogue item.
  • Once the remaining 70% is paid, you get complete handover: files, access, and ownership, in full.
  • If you do not respond to delivered work within 14 days, it is treated as accepted and the balance becomes due.
This protects both sides — you see exactly what you are paying for before the balance is due, and we are never left having delivered finished work with nothing paid against it.
06

Cancellation & refunds

  • Before work starts: cancel and the 30% upfront is refunded in full.
  • After work starts, before delivery: the upfront payment covers work already done and is non-refundable. You will receive whatever has been produced up to that point, as-is.
  • After delivery: the order is not refundable. If the delivered work does not match the agreed written scope, that is a revision and we will fix it at no charge — that is what the included revision rounds are for.
  • Monthly services: cancel any time. The current month is not pro-rated; service continues to the end of it.
  • If we cannot deliver what was agreed, you get a full refund of everything paid on that order.
  • Refunds are returned by the same method used to pay, where that is possible. Points spent on a refunded order are returned as Points.

Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of everything you give us — copy, data, branding, financial records.
  • You confirm you hold the rights to any material you upload, and that we may use it to deliver your order.
  • You will not use M&MCore for anything unlawful, deceptive, infringing, or intended to harm others.
  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure.
08

Intellectual property

  • Material you supply stays yours.
  • On full payment, the deliverables we produce for you become yours — you own the finished work outright.
  • Before full payment, we retain ownership of the work product.
  • We retain ownership of our own underlying tooling, agent frameworks, templates, and methods, which are not part of any deliverable unless a "framework handover" is explicitly what you ordered.
  • Third-party components (fonts, stock imagery, libraries, hosted services) remain under their own licences, which we will identify at handover.
  • We may reference the work in our portfolio unless you ask us in writing not to.
09

AI-generated output

Our deliverables are produced substantially by AI agents, under human review. You should read, check, and satisfy yourself with any deliverable before relying on it — particularly financial reports, feasibility studies, and marketing claims.

Nothing we deliver is legal, tax, accounting, financial, or investment advice. Bookkeeping and reporting services organise and present your data; they do not replace a licensed accountant or auditor, and we do not file anything on your behalf unless separately agreed in writing.

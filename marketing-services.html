Marketing services & pricing

Full AI marketing services, at M&MCore prices

A full AI marketing lineup -- built and run by agents, planned before anything is posted. À la carte below, or one of three packages further down.

Why this exists

Marketing, without a separate agency

Websites, design, bookkeeping, audits, custom agents -- and now marketing too, run by the same AI agents and handled from the same dashboard as everything else. One place for the parts of running a business that would otherwise mean juggling a handful of different vendors.

Still a real conversation first

Prices below are M&MCore's one-tier pricing, not blind checkout numbers — a real person confirms scope with you before any agent starts, exactly like every other M&MCore service.

These sit well above the light-touch Marketing Essentials on our core price list, and deliberately so: those are one-off assets or a simple posting cadence, while everything here is a continuously-run managed programme with real third-party media, data and compute behind it. On Paid Media items the price is our management fee — your ad spend goes straight to the platforms and is not included.

À la carte

Pick individual services

One standard price per service — no low/mid/high tier to compare. Anything marked monthly is billed for each month in advance and can be cancelled before the next one starts.

Or go all-in

Three packages

Two fixed monthly packages, and a third built entirely around what you actually need. Monthly packages are billed in full each month, in advance — not on the 30%/70% split we use for one-off project work, because there is no single completion to hold a balance against.

M&MCore Standard

One tier, every service

The only tier we offer — built to be a genuinely good default, not a stripped-down "budget" option.

  • Handled by our standard agent workflow, with a real person confirming scope before work starts
  • 2 free revision rounds, same as any tier elsewhere
  • 30% upfront, 70% on completion — no separate pricing model
  • The exact same deliverable quality bar on every order — nothing held back for a higher tier that doesn't exist

Why just one tier?

Splitting every service into low/mid/high tiers mostly just adds a decision to make before you can get started. M&MCore drops that: one clear scope, one price, described plainly before you pay anything. If your project genuinely needs more — a bigger build, a longer engagement — that's a conversation during scoping, not a tier upgrade to shop around.

The only things that change a price

There's no tier ladder, but there are three discounts, and all three are applied automatically — nothing to claim, enter, or ask for:

  • Business Pool — 20% off everything. Automatic once your lifetime spend passes $5,000. How it works →
  • Starter Package client — 50% off everything. Permanent, from the moment your Starter Package payment clears.
  • M&MCore Points — $1 off per Point. Earned from referrals and spendable on any order, up to 100% of the price. Full rules →
The two percentage discounts don't stack — if you qualify for both, you get the better one (50%), not 70% off. Points then come off whatever's left.

How you pay

One-off project work is 30% upfront, 70% on completion. Monthly services and marketing packages are billed in full for each month, in advance — cancel any time before the next month begins.

We take payment in USDT on BEP20. Place an order and you get the exact amount, the address and a QR code straight away. Read the full payment & delivery policy →

Who scopes the work?

You describe what you need, an AI agent asks a few follow-up questions, and a real person signs off on the final scope and price before anything starts. There's no tier to pick — just a scope to confirm.

Revisions work the same as anywhere else in the industry — two free rounds included on every order, no exceptions.

The Business Pool

A separate, elevated level of service for clients who work with us regularly — a real operational upgrade, not just a badge.

How you qualify

Qualification runs on your total spend with us, not how many separate tasks you've submitted — so it reflects real relationship value rather than order count.

  • Cross a cumulative spend threshold within a rolling period, or
  • Sign up directly for a monthly retainer covering ongoing work (marketing, maintenance, bookkeeping)
Once you qualify, you get a separate, dedicated dashboard view built specifically for Business Pool clients.

What actually changes

Once your spend with M&MCore crosses $5,000, your account automatically upgrades to Business Pool — no application, no approval to wait on. It just happens.

What changes:

  • A dedicated human manager — Business Pool accounts get a direct Telegram line to a real person assigned to your account, not the shared agent queue everyone else uses
  • Faster delivery on every request
  • 20% off all services
  • A design package included every month

Standard accounts stay connected through our AI agent specialist on Telegram — fast, always on, and fine for day-to-day requests. Business Pool swaps that for a dedicated human manager who already knows your account and history.

Why this exists

Most clients need one project done well and move on. Some need us running alongside their business continuously — marketing that doesn't stop, a site that stays current, books that stay clean without anyone chasing them. The Business Pool is built for that second kind of relationship, with the responsiveness and pricing to match.

Reach out and we'll check your eligibility — or start a monthly retainer directly.
